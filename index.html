About

Engineering for safer, smarter work

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student passionate about designing autonomous systems, intelligent machines and products that improve workplace safety and enhance human lives.

My mission is to use automation, robotics, machine design and product development to reduce human exposure to dangerous chemicals, sharp tools, repetitive tasks and physically demanding environments.

Through CAD modelling, simulation-driven design and hands-on prototyping, I turn complex engineering challenges into practical, reliable and scalable solutions.
